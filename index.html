Local alchemy

One distills. One knows the bar.

Red Eyed Spirits is two people: a chemist who rebuilt traditional recipes from first principles, and a hospitality veteran who knows what Rhode Island's best bars want to pour.

The chemist · Master distiller

Joey Steadman

A chemist and self-taught distiller who has built quality labs and production programs for spirits brands across Rhode Island.

George Tollefson

We source from our friends at New England farms, like local botanicals from Hawk and Handsaw Farm in Portsmouth and White Cap Flint Corn, Rhode Island's official state corn. Then we get it into the cocktail programs that care where things come from.
