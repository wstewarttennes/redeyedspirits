The spirits

Three bottles, rooted in tradition

Flagship · Double Gold, 99 pts

Saffron Rabarbaro Amaro

A modern twist on a Bolognese-style amaro

Delicate saffron and local botanicals from our friends at Hawk and Handsaw Farm in Portsmouth, Rhode Island.

"Opens with vibrant aromas of saffron and fresh herbs, unfolding into a richly herbaceous palate layered with floral notes and earthy roots, before finishing with a refreshing minty lift, delicate bitterness, and lingering saffron."2026 Tasting Alliance judge, 99 points
Silver

Amaro Caramino

A Milanese-style amaro, made to centuries-old tradition

Sweet and bitter orange peel, cherry bark and kola nut, with wormwood, grains of paradise, ginseng and 11 other botanicals. Naturally colored crimson with cochineal and sweetened with local honey and orange oleo saccharum.

"A distinctive, bittersweet amaro: blood orange, grapefruit, and classic wormwood-driven aperitif bitterness, with woody, saffron-like notes that add intrigue."2026 Tasting Alliance judge
Silver

Taffel Akvavit

A caraway spirit in the traditional Danish style

Earthy, nutty caraway with bright dill and pink peppercorn. Fennel and anise underneath, clementine zest, coriander and lavender on top. Say it "TAFF-uhl AH-kwah-veet."

"A clean and approachable aquavit: a silky palate with soft caraway and dry grain, before finishing with lingering pepper spice that carries well."2026 Tasting Alliance judge
