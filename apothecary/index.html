Saffron Rabarbaro Amaro

Rabarbaro is the Italian rhubarb amaro: earthy, bitter, a little smoky. Ours is lit up with saffron, so it pours gold and finishes warm.

Sip it neat, pour it over a big cube, or stir it into a Negroni in place of the Campari.
