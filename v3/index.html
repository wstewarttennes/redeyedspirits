The spirits

Three bottles. Three medals.

Every spirit in the line medaled at our first global competition.

Double Gold · 99 pts

Saffron Rabarbaro Amaro

A modern twist on a Bolognese-style amaro

Delicate saffron and local botanicals from Hawk and Handsaw Farm in Portsmouth, RI.

Vibrant aromas of saffron and fresh herbs... a refreshing minty lift, delicate bitterness, and lingering saffron. 2026 Tasting Alliance judge
Silver

Amaro Caramino

A Milanese-style amaro

Orange peel, cherry bark, kola nut, wormwood and 13 more botanicals. Colored with cochineal, sweetened with local honey.

Blood orange, grapefruit, and classic wormwood-driven aperitif bitterness. 2026 Tasting Alliance judge
Silver

Taffel Akvavit

Traditional Danish-style caraway spirit

Caraway, dill and pink peppercorn, lifted with clementine, coriander and lavender. "TAFF-uhl AH-kwah-veet."

A silky palate with soft caraway and dry grain, finishing with lingering pepper spice. 2026 Tasting Alliance judge
Local alchemy

Local botanicals. Tradition, rebuilt from first principles.

We work with our friends at New England farms to source ingredients this region knows and celebrates, like White Cap Flint Corn, Rhode Island's official state corn, and feature them in tradition-tested recipes.

Process designEvery step engineered from the chemistry up, not inherited by habit.
ReplicabilitySpecified, documented and validated so each batch matches the last.
